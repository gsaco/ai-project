# Literature check for the topic proposal

Review dated 11 October 2026. I use the literature to distinguish the established sunk-cost mechanism from the institutional experiment developed in this project. The review does not establish that no earlier paper studies the same comparison.

## What the proposed model contributes to this thesis

I develop a deterministic application: usable property and recurring institutional overhead affect operating profit; firms choose activity after equal one-period shocks; the resulting paths imply a conditional loss–gain inequality. An institutional label alone is not theoretical novelty. The literature check does not establish an original general theorem. The unresolved research task is the scope of the conditional comparison once suspension, entry, heterogeneous firms, and aggregation are allowed.

## Search boundary and next search

Queries included the exact Dixit title with “working paper”, “1988”, and “appendix”, Princeton-specific title searches, and subsequent work on hysteresis and policy. Rodrik was inspected for policy transmission; the closer 1992 Dixit follow-up was then added, including its published appendix. Searches also covered sunk-cost export entry, leading to the Roberts–Tybout author-institution abstract. A broader citation-chain search and recovery of unavailable materials remain tasks for the final paper. No correspondence was sent to authors.

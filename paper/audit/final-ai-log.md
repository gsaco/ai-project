# AI contributions and my assessment

I reviewed how the firm chooses its variable input, why the optimum is unique, what that implies for profit, and how the firm's choices depend on the assumptions. Two exchanges identify exactly where I questioned the AI's reasoning.

- In the slide discussion I quoted the step “Substitute and use `wx∗=q`” and asked to restore the missing factor α on slide 6. Multiplying the marginal condition by the optimal input gives wx* = αq. Thus the input bill uses only the share α of revenue. In the paper's example it is 0.40 on revenue of 0.80, leaving 0.40 before fixed expenses. I accept the corrected derivation. The correction appears in the [slide source](../../slides/final.tex).
- I originally suspected an error when a maximum in the value of closing became a minimum in the incentive to keep operating. My handwritten calculation confirmed that V − max{V − κ, 0} = min{κ, V}. My verdict on this step was “I accept it.” I also accepted the firm's operating and closure cases under the paper's assumptions. See my [handwritten calculation](../../hand/final/handwritten-revision-2026-11-09.png) and [original verdict](../../hand/final/verdict.md).

When asked about the GDP estimates or literature comparison, the suspension tie, the log example, the β = 0 counterexample, and the Lean proof, my response was “all good.” The entries marked **accepted as presented** refer to this collective verdict.

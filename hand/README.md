## Scope of the handwritten record

The first photograph gives the input optimum and the values of immediate and delayed restarting. The second uses these values to compare closure, continued operation, and suspension. At the impact threshold in Proposition 2, the handwritten equality of the output loss and gain applies to the operating choice. Closure is also optimal there and yields a strictly larger loss. Section 6 of the paper derives both outcomes.
